# NEXA v32 Reports Center

Replaces the old Reports redirect with a dedicated dense Reports Center based on the supplied reference.

Sections: Food Sale, Purchase & Inventory, Raw Material Sale.
Includes the requested Food Sale, Due Orders, Cancelled, Discounts, Review Reports, Menu Costing & Stats directory entries.
Report views use live NEXA Supabase data with date filters, CSV export and print. Menu Engineering is restricted to Developer/Admin.

No new SQL migration is required for this UI/report-query release.
